01.03.1 | Now, therefore, we shall expound the chapter called "Āragvadhīya".
01.03.2 | Thus spoke the venerable Ātreya.
01.03.3a | Āragvadha, karañja with eḍagaja, vāsā, guḍūcī, madana, the two haridrās; śryāhva, surāhva, khadira and dhava, nimba, viḍaṅga, the bark of karavīraka;
01.03.3b | ~
01.03.4a | the knot of bhūrja, laśuna, śirīṣa with lomaśa, guggulu and kṛṣṇagandhā; phaṇijjhaka, vatsaka and saptaparṇa, pīlu, kuṣṭha and the young shoots of sumanas;
01.03.4b | ~
01.03.5a | vacā, hareṇu, trivṛt, nikumbha, bhallātaka, red ochre (gairika) and añjana; manaḥśilā and āla, house soot (gṛhadhūma), elā, kāśīsa, lodhra, arjuna, musta and sarja —
01.03.5b | ~
01.03.6a | These six powder-plasters (cūrṇa-pradeha), thus set out in half-forms, soaked in cow's bile and ground again, mixed with mustard oil, are supremely proven; they are to be applied by the physician.
01.03.6b | ~
01.03.7a | When applied, they would quickly destroy in men the difficult kuṣṭhas (skin diseases), recent kilāsa, sureśalupta, kiṭibha with dadru, bhagandara and arśas, and apacī with pāmā.
01.03.7b | ~
01.03.8a | One should make powders, in equal parts, of kuṣṭha, the two haridrās, surasa, paṭola, nimba and aśvagandhā, suradāru and śigru, tumburu, dhānya and vanya with sarṣapa, and caṇḍā;
01.03.8b | ~
01.03.9a | With these, ground in buttermilk (takra), one should first try to rub (udvartana) the body anointed with oil; by that his itching (kaṇḍū), eruptions (piḍakā) with koṭha, kuṣṭhas and swellings (śopha) subside.
01.03.9b | ~
01.03.10a | Kuṣṭha, amṛtāsaṅga, kaṭaṅkaṭerī, kāsīsa, kampillaka, musta and lodhra; saugandhika, the resin of sarja, viḍaṅga, manaḥśilā and āla, the bark of karavīraka —
01.03.10b | ~
01.03.11a | These prepared powders should be applied for dusting (avacūrṇana) to one whose limbs are anointed with oil; thus dadru with itching, kiṭibha, pāmā and vicarcikā also come to subsidence.
01.03.11b | ~
01.03.12a | Manaḥśilā and āla, marica, oil and the latex (payas) of arka are a plaster that removes kuṣṭha; tuttha, viḍaṅga, marica, kuṣṭha and lodhra, with manaḥśilā, would likewise be so.
01.03.12b | ~
01.03.13a | Rasāñjana with prapunāḍa seeds, mixed with the juice of kapittha, is a paste (lepa); and karañja seed and eḍagaja with kuṣṭha, ground in cow's urine, is an excellent plaster (pradeha).
01.03.13b | ~
01.03.14a | One should make a paste (lepa) of both haridrās, the seed of kuṭaja, karañja seed, the young shoots of sumanas and the bark of hayamāraka with its middle part, mixed with sesame alkali (tila-kṣāra).
01.03.14b | ~
01.03.15a | Manaḥśilā, the bark of kuṭaja with kuṣṭha, karañja with lomaśa and eḍagaja, the knot of bhūrja, and the root of karavīra: these powders are to be prepared with tuṣodaka,
01.03.15b | ~
01.03.16a | and also with the liquid of burnt palāśa measuring one āḍhaka, taken a karṣa each; this paste, smearing the ladle (darvī-pralepa), they declare supreme for destroying kuṣṭha.
01.03.16b | ~
01.03.17a | Having ground the leaves of caturaṅgula with buttermilk, then the leaves of kākamācī, and with the leaves of aśvahana, one should rub (udvartana) the kuṣṭhas of a man whose limbs are anointed with oil.
01.03.17b | ~
01.03.18a | Kola, kulattha, suradāru, rāsnā, māṣa, atasī and oil-seeds, kuṣṭha, vacā, śatāhvā, barley flour and amla (sour): warm, these are a plaster for those suffering from Vāta diseases.
01.03.18b | ~
01.03.19a | A plaster with warm vesavāra of marsh (ānūpa) fish and flesh would remove Vāta (pavana); a plaster with the four fats (sneha) mixed with daśamūla, and with fragrant drugs, destroys Vāta (anila).
01.03.19b | ~
01.03.20a | Warm barley flour mixed with buttermilk, with alkali (kṣāra), would destroy pain in the abdomen; kuṣṭha and śatāhvā with vacā, and the flour of barley, with oil and amla (sour), they recommend in Vāta.
01.03.20b | ~
01.03.21a | In Vāta (pavana) with blood (rakta), one should make a plaster of both śatāhvās, madhuka, madhūka, balā, priyāla and kaśeruka, ghee, vidārī and sugar candy (sitopalā).
01.03.21b | ~
01.03.22a | Rāsnā, guḍūcī, madhuka, the two balās, with jīvaka and ṛṣabhaka, and milk; and ghee, cooked (siddha) and mixed with beeswax (madhuśeṣa): as a plaster it would dispel the pain of rakta and Vāta (anila).
01.03.22b | ~
01.03.23a | In Vāta with rakta, wheat flour and goat's milk with ghee is a plaster; nata and utpala, mixed with candana and kuṣṭha, with ghee, is a plaster in headache.
01.03.23b | ~
01.03.24a | Prapauṇḍarīka, suradāru, kuṣṭha, yaṣṭyāhva, elā, kamala and utpala, and with loha, erakā, padmaka and coraka: a plaster with ghee in headache.
01.03.24b | ~
01.03.25a | Rāsnā, the two haridrās, nalada, the two śatāhvās, devadāru, sitopalā and the root of jīvantī, with ghee and with oil, lukewarm, is an ointment (ālepana) in pains of the sides.
01.03.25b | ~
01.03.26a | Śaivāla, padma, utpala, vetra, tuṅga, prapauṇḍarīka, amṛṇāla and lodhra, priyaṅgu, kāleyaka and candana: with ghee, the plaster would be cooling (nirvāpaṇa).
01.03.26b | ~
01.03.27a | Sitā, latā, vetasa and padmaka, yaṣṭyāhva, aindrī, nalinas, dūrvā, the root of yavāsa and of kuśa and kāśa, jala and erakā: this would be cooling (nirvāpaṇa).
01.03.27b | ~
01.03.28a | Śaileya, elā and aguru with kuṣṭha, caṇḍā, nata, tvak, suradāru and rāsnā: as a plaster it would quickly destroy cold; and śirīṣa with sindhuvāra, poison.
01.03.28b | ~
01.03.29a | Rubbing (pragharṣa) with śirīṣa, lāmajjaka, hema and lodhra removes skin disorders (tvag-doṣa) and profuse sweating (saṃsveda); patra, ambu, lodhra, abhaya and candana are a plaster that removes bad odour of the body.
01.03.29b | ~
01.03.30a | Here is a verse: here, in the Āragvadhīya, the son of Atri, revered by siddhas and great sages, taught, for the welfare of the world, thirty-two most proven powder-plasters (cūrṇa-pradeha) that destroy various diseases.
01.03.30b | ~
01.03.30c | ~
01.03.P1 | Thus, in the Śloka section (Ślokasthāna) of the treatise composed by Agniveśa and redacted by Caraka, the third chapter, named Āragvadhīya.
