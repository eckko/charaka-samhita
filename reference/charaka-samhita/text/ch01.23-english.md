01.23.1 | Now, therefore, we shall expound the chapter on replenishing (Saṃtarpaṇīya).
01.23.2 | Thus said the venerable Ātreya.
01.23.3a | One who replenishes himself with unctuous, sweet, heavy and slimy (foods), with new grain and new wine, and with the meats of marsh and water (animals),
01.23.3b | ~
01.23.4a | with milk products, with foods made of guḍa and with flour preparations, in excessive measure, who is averse to exertion and delights in the comfort of day-sleep, bed and seat,
01.23.4b | ~
01.23.5a | in him arise the diseases caused by replenishing (saṃtarpaṇa): prameha, eruptions (piḍakā), koṭha (rashes), itching, pāṇḍu disease and fever,
01.23.5b | ~
01.23.6a | kuṣṭhas, disorders of āma (undigested matter), mūtrakṛcchra, loss of taste (arocaka), drowsiness, impotence, excessive corpulence, laziness, heaviness of the limbs,
01.23.6b | ~
01.23.7a | coating of the senses and channels, clouding of the intellect, stupor (pramīlaka), swellings and others of this kind — in one who does not quickly counteract (them).
01.23.7b | ~
01.23.8a | In that case vomiting (ullekhana), purgation, bloodletting, exercise, fasting, medicated smoking (dhūma) and sweatings are commended,
01.23.8b | ~
01.23.9a | the licking of abhayā with honey, mostly the use of dry food, and the powder-plasters that have been stated as destroying itching and koṭha.
01.23.9b | ~
01.23.10a | One should drink triphalā and āragvadha, pāṭhā, saptaparṇa with vatsaka, musta with madana, and nimba, boiled in water.
01.23.10b | ~
01.23.11a | By that, administered with due measure and time, meha and the other (disorders) arising from replenishing surely perish in one who uses it regularly.
01.23.11b | ~
01.23.12a | Musta, āragvadha, pāṭhā, triphalā and devadāru, śvadaṃṣṭrā, khadira, nimba, the two haridrās, and bark from vatsaka:
01.23.12b | ~
01.23.13a | A man who drinks the juice of these every morning, according to the doṣa, is freed from all diseases caused by replenishing.
01.23.13b | ~
01.23.14a | And by these, employed in applications of rubbing (udvartana), friction (udgharṣa) and bathing, and likewise combined with unctuous substance (sneha), skin disorders subside.
01.23.14b | ~
01.23.15a | Kuṣṭha, gomedaka, hiṅgu, bone of the krauñca, the three pungents (tryūṣaṇa), vacā, vṛṣaka and elā, śvadaṃṣṭrā, kharāhvā and aśmabhedaka:
01.23.15b | ~
01.23.16a | This, drunk with buttermilk, with dadhimaṇḍa (the clear part of curd) or with the sour juice of badara, removes mūtrakṛcchra and prameha.
01.23.16b | ~
01.23.17a | By administrations of buttermilk and abhayā, and in just the same way (by those) of triphalā, and by administrations of ariṣṭas, meha and the others subside.
01.23.17b | ~
01.23.18a | Tryūṣaṇa, triphalā, honey, krimighna, ajamodaka, saktu (roasted flour) and oil: this mantha, steeped in metal-water, is beneficial.
01.23.18b | ~
01.23.19a | Vyoṣa, viḍaṅga, śigru, triphalā, kaṭurohiṇī, the two bṛhatīs, the two haridrās, pāṭhā, ativiṣā, sthirā,
01.23.19b | ~
01.23.20a | hiṅgu, roots of kebuka, yavānī, dhānya and citraka, sauvarcala (salt), ajājī and hapuṣā: these one should powder.
01.23.20b | ~
01.23.21a | The portions of powder, oil, ghee and honey should be equal by measure, and the portion of saktu sixteenfold; one should drink (this) replenishing preparation (saṃtarpaṇa).
01.23.21b | ~
01.23.22a | From the use of this the diseases arising from replenishing subside: pramehas, obstructed Vāta (mūḍhavāta), kuṣṭhas, arśas, kāmalās,
01.23.22b | ~
01.23.23a | spleen disorder (plīhā), pāṇḍu disease, swelling, mūtrakṛcchra, loss of taste, heart disease, rājayakṣman, cough, dyspnoea, galagraha,
01.23.23b | ~
01.23.24a | worms (krimi), grahaṇī disorders, śvaitrya (whiteness of skin) and excessive corpulence; and men's digestive fire is kindled, and memory and intellect increase.
01.23.24b | ~
01.23.25a | One who is constant in exercise, eats after (the previous meal) is digested, and has barley and wheat for food, sheds corpulence and is freed from the disorders caused by replenishing.
01.23.25b | ~
01.23.26a | Depletion (apatarpaṇa), the medicine for those (diseases) arising from replenishing (saṃtarpaṇa), has been stated; hereafter the diseases born of depletion will be stated with their medicines.
01.23.26b | ~
01.23.27a | Wasting of body, digestive fire, strength, complexion, ojas (vital essence), semen and flesh; fever, persistence of cough, pain in the sides, loss of taste,
01.23.27b | ~
01.23.28a | weakness of hearing, insanity, delirious talk, pain in the heart, retention of faeces and urine, pain located in the shanks, thighs and trika (lower back),
01.23.28b | ~
01.23.29a | breaking pain in the small joints (parvan), bones and joints, and the other diseases born of Vāta, ūrdhvavāta (upward Vāta) and the rest: all these arise from depletion.
01.23.29b | ~
01.23.30a | For them, those who know it have declared replenishing (saṃtarpaṇa), in turn, as the medicine: that which may be effective at once or with continued use is wanted.
01.23.30b | ~
01.23.31a | For one wasted suddenly is indeed built up quickly by tarpaṇa (replenishing); but one wasted over a long time does not thrive without continued replenishing (saṃtarpaṇa).
01.23.31b | ~
01.23.32a | For one long weak, the medicine is to be given without haste by one who pays regard to the body, digestive fire, doṣa, medicine, measure and time.
01.23.32b | ~
01.23.33a | Beneficial for him are meat-juices, milks and ghees, baths, enemas (basti), oil massages and those which are tarpaṇas (replenishing preparations).
01.23.33b | ~
01.23.34a | For those afflicted with fever and cough, the emaciated, those with mūtrakṛcchra, the thirsty and those with upward Vāta (ūrdhvavāta), beneficial tarpaṇas (replenishing preparations) will be described.
01.23.34b | ~
01.23.35a | For them is commended an aphrodisiac mantha with sugar, pippalī, oil, ghee and honey in equal parts, and saktu doubled.
01.23.35b | ~
01.23.36a | Saktu, madirā, honey and sugar: this tarpaṇa (replenishing preparation) one should drink; it moves Vāta (māruta), faeces, urine, Kapha and Pitta downward.
01.23.36b | ~
01.23.37a | Phāṇita, saktu, ghee, dadhimaṇḍa and sour kāñjika: one should drink this tarpaṇa (replenishing preparation), which destroys mūtrakṛcchra and removes udāvarta.
01.23.37b | ~
01.23.38a | A mantha combined with kharjūra, mṛdvīkā, vṛkṣāmla, amlīka and dāḍima, and with parūṣaka together with āmalaka, dispels the disorders of wine (madya).
01.23.38b | ~
01.23.39a | Sweet or sour, made with water, with unctuous substance or just dry, the mantha is at once replenishing (saṃtarpaṇa) and gives firmness, complexion and strength.
01.23.39b | ~
01.23.40a | Here, the verse: The diseases that arise from replenishing and the diseases that (arise) from depletion have been set forth, with their medicines, in the chapter Saṃtarpaṇīya.
01.23.40b | ~
01.23.40c | ~
01.23.P1 | Thus, in the treatise composed by Agniveśa and redacted by Caraka, in the Śloka section (Ślokasthāna), the twenty-third chapter named Saṃtarpaṇīya.
